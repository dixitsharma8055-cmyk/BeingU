# Information needed from you for the legal documents

I have not invented any company detail, registration number, address, officer name, or legal declaration. Each item below must come from you or your counsel. Put the answers in a private document (not in the repository).

## Company
- [ ] Legal name of the company that operates BeingU, exactly as registered
- [ ] Type of entity (for example, private limited company or LLP)
- [ ] Registration number (CIN or LLPIN) and the registering authority
- [ ] Registered office address
- [ ] GST number, if registered
- [ ] Country of incorporation (confirm: India)

## Contacts
- [ ] Grievance officer: name and designation (required under the IT Rules, 2021, as amended)
- [ ] Grievance email address that is actively monitored
- [ ] A postal address for legal notices, if different from the registered office
- [ ] General support email for users
- [ ] Whether you will appoint a Data Protection Officer (counsel to confirm whether the DPDP Act requires one for BeingU)

## Legal
- [ ] Courts with jurisdiction for disputes (city)
- [ ] Publication date for each final document
- [ ] Qualified Indian lawyer or firm you will use for review, with their sign-off recorded

## Data and processors (counsel and engineering to confirm)
- [ ] Every service that processes personal data, and where it runs (Supabase in Mumbai; SMS provider; crash reporting; analytics)
- [ ] Any processor outside India, and the safeguards in place
- [ ] Retention periods: logs, moderation records, reports, backups, deleted-account data
- [ ] Whether analytics needs a separate, optional consent

## Product decisions
- [ ] Age check approach (self-declared date of birth today; is more needed?)
- [ ] Appeal window in days
- [ ] Whether BeingU will ever show advertising or sponsored content (the advertising policy is a draft for that case)
- [ ] Whether messages are kept after an account is deleted, and for how long
- [ ] Whether saved items exist (the deletion draft refers to them; remove the reference if they do not)

## Review gaps (what is incomplete today)
- Privacy Policy sections 6 and 7 still need counsel's processor list and retention periods.
- Terms section 10 needs the court city.
- Moderation Policy sections 4, 7, 8 and 9 need counsel's escalation, appeal, retention, and takedown details.
- Deletion Policy sections 3, 5 and 4 need counsel's retention and backup details, and messaging details once messaging exists.
- The grievance officer and publication date are placeholders in every document.
- All documents carry a draft banner in the app until they are approved.
